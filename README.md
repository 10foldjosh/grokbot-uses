## New / Novel Use Cases from Run 13

### Advanced Finance, Research Desks & Wealth Management
- **Multi-bot portfolio monitoring / research desk (non-trading)**: 6 specialized bots track earnings, read new filings, update valuations, check options, challenge thesis, and compile into one morning report. Copy-paste prompts available. Source: @JasonL_Capital on X (Sep 2026, high engagement ~40k views).
- **Crypto research desk with 6 specialist agents (no live trading)**: Scanner (new pairs/repos/volume), Risk (contract/mint/LP lock), Whale (smart money accumulation flags), Rug (dev wallet 24/7 LP touch alerts), Buzz (social speed as signal only), Head of Desk (routes, handoffs, one human call). Overnight: scanned 371 tokens, blocked 38 scams/honeypots, left one token + full log. Each on own cloud computer; morning Telegram. Source: @mintzdev on X (Nov 19, 2026).
- **Co-Invest MCP for portfolio analysis + approved trades**: Connect Grok Bot to Liquid via MCP (https://coinvest.liquid.trade/mcp). Analyzes positions, market positioning, proposes trades based on news + existing book, sizes/places after confirmation, reports fills in-chat. Source: @frank_liquid on X (Aug 2026) + official integration notes.
- **Paper-trading AI hedge fund simulation**: Multi-agent team (analysts for SEC filings/supply chain/calendar, clerks, bookkeeper, tax specialist, trader) + chief of staff. Benchmarks vs S&P 500; explicit paper-only, not advice. Source: mindstudio.ai demo coverage (Oct 2026).
- **Personal finance exception catcher**: Daily bank check flags unexpected charges (e.g., $14 Disney+ after cancel) and unsubscribes in minutes. Source: @TonyChanBuilds on X (Nov 19, 2026).
- **Official FinServ workshop patterns**: SpaceXSI demos for wealth management (exposure/positions bots, IC memos/decks, comms policy checks) with controls for regulated firms (auto-review, audit, isolated computers). Source: YouTube "Grok Bot for Financial Services" workshop (Nov 2026).

### Senior Care, Aging-in-Place & Longevity
- **Senior Care Helper**: Tracks medicines, appointments, refills for older adults; prepares family updates for approval. Community template. Source: swarm.school / Kelly Baltzell.
- **Longevity Grok / PLSI (open-source multi-agent on Grok Bot)**: By Insilico Medicine + David Sinclair. Unifies labs/wearables into personal health trajectory, proposes scored interventions, supports opt-in research. Physician prompts: patient mail coding, clinic sync, panel overview, ward bots (one patient/one bot), session rooms, plain-language reply drafts (approve before send). Install via plsi.md from longevitygrok.si. Source: @precious_gpt / Insilico on X (Nov 2026), regeneration.ai, official site.
- **Caregiving multi-bot setups**: Rolling Rogans podcast details bots for caregiving, health tracking, energy pacing, anxiety reduction while caring for family (e.g., dementia). Voice + routines + camera/watch integrations. Source: Apple Podcasts "ART OF AGING" episodes (Sep 2026).

### Small Service Business Ops
- **Mass client rebooking for non-tech salon owner**: After surprise schedule change, Grok Bot rebooked ~70 nail salon clients in ~1 hour using Google Calendar history + WhatsApp. Setup/prompt shared. Source: @botpagesco / @ShuiCohen on X (Nov 19, 2026) + botpages.co use-case page.
