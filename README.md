## New / Novel Use Cases from Run 12

### E-commerce, Shopify & Retail Back-Office / Customer Ops
- **Full end-to-end customer service for Shopify small business**: Bot reads inbox, checks orders in Shopify, tracks every case in Notion, replies to customers on returns, exchanges, shipping questions, and follow-ups. User only signs off on big calls. Source: @RbyCharles on X (Nov 15, 2026).
- **Licensing reports automation from Shopify**: Product maker uses bot connected to Shopify to gather data and plug into report templates for licensing deals. Reduced from ~1 hour of manual gathering/plugging to 5 minutes per deal; bot flagged warehouse transfers incorrectly marked as sold. Source: @twalkerp on X (Nov 17, 2026).
- **Automated restocking across 70+ vendors + CS drafts + listings**: Bot drafts customer service emails, updates product listings on Shopify, and handles restocking orders across 70+ vendors — mundane tasks that previously took hours. Source: @carp_or_die on X (Nov 17, 2026).
- **Official Shopify connector for orders, inventory, listings**: Grok Bot can connect to Shopify to check orders, track inventory, and keep product listings up to date (announced Oct 2026 by @bot). Community guides detail 6 specialized bots (Shopping/Merchant Center ROAS, Meta ads with inventory sync, Creative from product photos, Store SEO for meta/alt/canonicals, etc.) with approval gates for bigger changes. Sources: official @bot post, grokbot.sh blog (Sep 2026), dtcskills.com deep-dive on read/write scopes and safe first jobs (days of cover by SKU).
- **Shopify templates for inventory/orders/tracking**: Public templates include Shopify Bot (coach + weekday brief), Shopify Store Manager (daily watch of orders/payments/inventory/checkout, alerts only when unhealthy), Tracking Tuna Officer (weekly tracking checks to spreadsheet), OPSY (customer-care email/site-chat follow-ups). Source: botshophq.com guides.
- **Early agentic commerce signals**: Global-e Q3 2026 earnings noted AI-sourced browsing and checkout volumes approximately doubled sequentially; orders beginning to arrive through AI agents including Grok Bot (still small volume). Source: @EmmanuelInvest summary of Global-e earnings call (Nov 18, 2026).
