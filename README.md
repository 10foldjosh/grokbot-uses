## Novel / Productive Use Cases

### Business & Operations
- **Support queue triage at scale**: SpaceXAI used Grok Bot to spot incidents and clear ~204 tickets in ~10 minutes (engineer approved batch). Source: official reports / Sumus summary.
- **Vendor spend audit**: One bot identified >$100k in unused software seats and purchases. Source: newsletter/SpaceXSI reports.
- **Outbound agency automation**: Growth Engine X uses bots to build Clay workflows and review campaigns daily; positive responses increased significantly. Source: aiusecasesforbusiness.com.
- **Mortgage CRM self-updating**: Loan officer built Loan-Bot that pulls from LOS/Outlook and flags stalled apps. Source: aiusecasesforbusiness.com.
- **Personal-training payroll**: Sub-agents check sessions vs payments and push timesheets to Xero (20+ staff). Source: aiusecasesforbusiness.com.
- **Pokemon/eBay listing from photos**: Valued binders from photos, listed ~200 items, generated $2.6k in orders. Source: usinggrokbot.com / Reddit.
- **Plumbing / field service business run**: Handles emails (AOL/Yahoo), scheduling, etc. Source: Reddit via usinggrokbot.com.
- **Multi-business coordination**: Separate bots per business (truck wash, hood cleaning, etc.) coordinated by a manager bot that escalates. Source: X / agentsinuse.com.
- **Realtor admin team**: Canadian realtor built multi-bot system (with the bot) for listings hunt, CMA drafts, follow-ups, listing copy, offer notes, monthly reports; wires into CRMs/forms; shareable template. Human approval on all external sends. Source: X (@danielfoch) / Homies AI research write-up.
- **High-volume media buyer ops**: Manages $200K+/day affiliate/ecom ad spend across Google Ads, YouTube, Everflow, etc. Hourly margin checks (real revenue vs reported), cut/scale rules, early flag of losing ads, lead quality matching, transcript-to-performance analysis, cash flow/bank matching. Source: X (@runtraffic).

- **Newsletter detox + unsubscribe**: Audits subs, presents keep/unsubscribe page, handles unsub with approval. Source: grokbot.dev.
- **Credit-card optimizer**: Picks best card for points/cash-back and tracks expiring credits. Source: Sumus / Marketplace.

- **Unclaimed property & subscription savings**: Found $200 unclaimed + $700/year sub savings. Source: X.
- **Daily YouTube / list-based news digests**: Summarizes channels or X lists into personalized pages. Source: grokbot.dev / X.
