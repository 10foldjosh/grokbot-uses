## New / Novel Use Cases from Run 6

### Legal & Professional Services
- **Solo attorney back-office automation**: In ~20 minutes, a solo practitioner had Grok Bot clean 20,000+ old emails, draft/send a reply on a new case, calendar an arraignment, open a new case folder in OneDrive, and place the indictment in the filing folder. Described as a game-changer for practices with no employees (calendaring, filing, organization). Source: X post by @RTradesX (Nov 11, 2026).
- **Court portal file clerk**: Lawyer taught a bot a difficult court e-filing portal; it pulled and first-read 70 pending matters, producing case-wise summaries for a next-day presentation (finished in ~1 hour from list receipt). Source: Lawyer posts on X (e.g., around Oct 2026).
- **Small Claims & Complaint Filer**: Public bot that drafts demand letters, escalates to supervisors/card disputes, files consumer complaints, and explains small-claims process with pros/cons/costs. All drafts for human review/send; tracks deadlines. Not legal advice. Source: @BrianRoemmele on X (Nov 4, 2026).
- **Legal Ops workflows** (official + reported): Counsel 1:1 prep and writeback from Slack/Notion/notes; Slack triage for consistent guidance; Legal Review tracker refresh with deal context; contract lifecycle support (lookups, summaries, guided moves); weekly executive drafts. Humans review all outbound. Source: Official x.ai/bot guides and related posts.

### Insurance, Healthcare Admin & Utilities
- **Insurance claim filing & follow-up**: Bots gather photos/reports/receipts/emails, draft claims, log every call/email/cost, and prepare appeals on rejection. Multiple reported cases with savings (e.g., travel insurance phone replacement free, car claims closed). Source: paidforitself.com and X reports.
- **Prescription coverage & insurance calls**: Users directing bots to call insurers to check coverage; blocked by some carriers requiring live human approval or refusing authorized-user status. Ideas for voice merge/transfer or pre-recorded verified authorization. Source: Recent X posts (Nov 12, 2026).
- **Electric bill / rate monitoring**: Bot that checks rates monthly and reminds when contract is up for renewal (shared template). Source: X post with downloadable bot link.

### Outdoor / Recreation
- **Hunting land scout**: Overnight agent work through Colorado parcel records to find private land bordering public hunting ground. Turned up 78,670 properties and a working list of 3,393 landowners by midday. Highly creative use of public records + browser/computer. Source: @BigGameScouts / Bot Pages on X (Nov 12, 2026).

### Hardware / Wearables
- **Smart glasses integration**: User created a plugin so Grok Bot works on Even G2 glasses (alongside an existing Hermes plugin). Hands-free interaction described as "pretty slick." Source: X post by @dalethomas50 (Nov 12, 2026).

### Other Productive Patterns Observed
- Multi-bot coordination and rule consistency (bot checks new rules against existing memories and updates old ones).
- Official high-level roles still heavily used: Chief of Staff, Expense Manager, Sales Outbound, Talent Scout, Paid Media, Account Health, etc. (see x.ai/bot/use-cases).
- Strong presence in money/bills (refunds, subscription negotiation, unclaimed property), life admin, and software/DevOps.
