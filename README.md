## New / Novel Use Cases from Run 7

### Agriculture & Ranching
- **Cattle ranch multi-bot team for ops & meetings**: Retired Navy Captain and owner-operator of B&A Ranch (cow-calf operation) runs a Grok Bot team including Chief of Staff (notes), Ranch Manager, Financial Manager (numbers), and Maintenance Officer. Handles morning meetings, receipts, hay numbers, and photo sorting (a week of sorting done in five minutes). Frees the owner to stay outside with the cattle. Source: X posts by @billcox1962 (Nov 13, 2026).

### Education & Parent/Teacher Workflows
- **Teacher drafting helper (student-data-free)**: Teachers use Grok Bot for lesson plans (from outline + standard, with timing/warmup), quizzes, rubrics, research with sources, and weekly plans. Explicit guidance to keep out rosters, grades, IEPs, named student work, or class photos; no school system logins unless approved. Source: jonathanmontoya.com guide (Nov 2026); botcookbook.com teacher bots.
- **School inbox checklist per kid**: Parent (Stella) used bot on back-to-school emails and household to-dos for multiple kids. Splits into kid-specific checklists (forms, supplies, dates, who signs); handles independent household jobs. High engagement (787 likes). Shareable prompt on grokbot.dev. Source: grokbot.dev/use-cases/back-to-school-mental-load.
- **Tutoring & study bots**: Shared bots like Tutor, Professor G (step-by-step courses with analogies + checks), Language Coach, Tutoring Assistant for explanations, practice problems, SAT/ACT plans. Parent homework coaching with Socratic prompts, assignment tracking from school email/calendar. Source: botcookbook.com, thebutlergrokbot.com.
- **Education nonprofit outreach**: Worldwide Studios (education nonprofit) used Grok Bot for outreach/research on a community college robotics hack at CCSF. 10x'd signups from a couple to over 40 in one week. Source: X post by @michaelraspuzzi (Nov 9-10, 2026).

### Nonprofits & Community
- **Small nonprofit drafts (reversible jobs)**: Guides emphasize one reversible job first — thank-you copy drafts from gift CSVs, board briefing packs. Never automate donate forms or CRM writes; human review. Source: botskills.sh guide.
- **Community workshops & hackathons**: Meetups and events teaching Grok Bot setup for nonprofits/small business (follow-ups, donor thanks). London community engineering hackathon focused on Grok Bot + Cursor for community tooling. Source: Meetup, Luma events.

### Real Estate
- **Agent lead & listing workflows**: Drafts-first bots for new buyer/seller lead replies (qualifying questions, calendar times), quiet-lead follow-ups, listing descriptions from notes/photos, market research tables. Human approval before any send. Templates on grokbot.dev (Realtor watcher for new listings + comps/schools/permits), Homies AI shared template (onboarding, showings, CMAs, CRM integrations). Source: whatshouldmybotdo.com, grokbot.dev, homiesai.com, realtyhack.com (Austin brokers: AI receptionist, idea bot, transaction coordinator).
- **Buyer research for risks**: Bot researches listings for title issues, pending lawsuits, flood plain mislabels, square footage discrepancies, unpermitted work, water violations, geological dangers, future builds affecting value/safety. Source: Recent X post (Nov 13, 2026).

### Creative Arts, Media & Content Pipelines
- **Podcast & video production pipelines**: Shared bots for full podcast pipelines (research topic, multi-host scripts, speech stitching), explainer video studios (cited research + Grok Imagine + voice + QA + upload), horror shorts, content idea scanners from X. Orchestrator patterns for design + content bots. Source: grokbot.zone, grokbots.de, Startup Ideas Podcast episode with Ras Mic (engineering orchestrator, content bot scanning X for topics).

### Other Productive Patterns Observed
- Multi-role specialist teams adapted to vertical ops (ranch, real estate brokerage, education nonprofit).
- Strong emphasis on drafts-for-approval and human-in-loop for any external communication.
- Shareable templates and marketplace bots accelerating adoption in new domains.
